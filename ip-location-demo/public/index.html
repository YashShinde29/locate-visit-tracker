How it works

IP
Approximate location (usually the city) is read on the server, no key needed.
GPS
Precise location needs the visitor to tap Allow — the browser enforces this.
Saved
Every visit is stored and shown on your dashboard, where you can search and delete it.
Use it responsibly. Reading someone's location without telling them can break privacy laws and platform rules. Use links with people who know what they do, and don't track anyone without consent.
